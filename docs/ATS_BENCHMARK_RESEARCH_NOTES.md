## Initial benchmark lens

The initial comparison groups functionality into ten domains: requisition and workforce planning; sourcing and CRM; candidate experience and career sites; pipeline, collaboration, and structured interviewing; offers and onboarding handoff; communication and scheduling; reporting and operations; integration ecosystem; security, privacy, and global compliance; and governed AI controls. Features that require organization-owned messaging, identity, job-board, HRIS, assessment, background-check, calendar, or e-signature credentials must remain implementation-ready but inactive until administrators provide the approved configuration.
